Course description

Math 210B continues the two-quarter development of modern real analysis in Euclidean spaces.

The Winter quarter studies differentiation and integration in greater depth, including monotone functions, functions of bounded variation, absolutely continuous functions, differentiation almost everywhere, the Lebesgue differentiation theorem, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus in the Lebesgue setting. The course then develops Lp spaces, Hölder's and Minkowski's inequalities, completeness, approximation and density results, and selected aspects of L2 theory and multiple integration.

Course perspective. Math 210A and 210B are intended as a coherent sequence centered on the main analytical ideas in Euclidean spaces. Systematic extension to general measure spaces may be treated subsequently.

Tentative weekly outline

Three one-hour lectures per week. The first lecture reviews the Fall quarter and gives an overview of the Winter quarter. The schedule may be adjusted as the course develops.

Week 1 · Overview and bounded variation
  • Review and overview
  • Functions of bounded variation
  • Monotone functions and BV decomposition
Week 2 · Differentiability almost everywhere
  • Differentiability of monotone functions
  • Almost-everywhere differentiability
  • Examples and counterexamples
Week 3 · Absolute continuity
  • Absolutely continuous functions
  • Relations among Lipschitz, AC, and BV functions
  • Basic structural properties
Week 4 · Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
  • Indefinite integrals
  • Differentiation of indefinite integrals
  • FTC in the Lebesgue setting
Week 5 · Lebesgue differentiation
  • Lebesgue differentiation theorem
  • Applications
  • Synthesis of differentiation, BV, AC, and FTC
Week 6 · Introduction to Lp spaces
  • Lp spaces and equivalence classes
  • Hölder's inequality
  • Basic estimates
Week 7 · Minkowski and completeness
  • Minkowski's inequality
  • Completeness of Lp
  • Relations among Lp spaces
Week 8 · Approximation and density
  • Approximation by simple functions
  • Density results
  • Modes of convergence in Lp
Week 9 · L2 and applications
  • L2 as an inner-product space
  • Orthogonality
  • Applications of Lp estimates
Week 10 · Multiple integration and synthesis
  • Fubini and Tonelli on Euclidean spaces
  • Integration on Rn
  • Review and transition to abstract measure theory
